# Bluesky limits in Magpie

## Why a move can pause

Bluesky counts every follow, block, list, and list member that Magpie adds as one write.
Each account may write only so much in an hour and in a day.
Magpie keeps under those limits, so your new account is not flagged.
Sometimes Bluesky also asks Magpie to wait.
Nothing is wrong with your account or with the app.

## How many items

Bluesky gives each account 5,000 points an hour and 35,000 a day, and every add costs 3 points.
That is about 1,666 adds an hour and 11,666 a day.
Magpie moves at most 500 items a minute, 1,500 an hour, and 10,000 a day.
The rest of the points are left for your own posts and likes.
Items are the people you follow, the people you block, your lists and their members, and your mutes and saved feeds.
When Bluesky asks Magpie to wait, Magpie waits, even if it moved fewer.

## Why a big move takes more than one session

Up to about 1,500 items fit in one hour, which takes about three minutes.
A move with more items than that stops and shows "Later" on the items that are still waiting.
If you said yes to reminders, Magpie tells you when you can move the rest.

## What is safe

Items that already moved stay moved.
Open Magpie and tap Continue to move the rest.
Nothing is lost, and no item moves twice.
