# GitHub limits in Magpie

## Why a move can pause

GitHub's rules do not allow starring or following a lot in a short time.
Magpie goes slowly to keep your account safe.
Sometimes GitHub also asks Magpie to wait.
Nothing is wrong with your account or with the app.

## How many items

Magpie moves at most 20 items per minute and 300 items per hour.
Items are your stars, star lists, starred gists, and the people you follow.
When GitHub asks Magpie to wait, Magpie waits, even if it moved fewer.

## When the move can go on

Usually within the hour.
Magpie shows "Later" on the items that are still waiting.
If you said yes to reminders, Magpie tells you when you can move the rest.

## What is safe

Items that already moved stay moved.
Open Magpie and tap Continue to move the rest.
Nothing is lost, and no item moves twice.
