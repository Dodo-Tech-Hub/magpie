# YouTube limits in Magpie

## Why a move can pause

YouTube gives each app a fixed amount of work per day.
All people who use Magpie share this amount.
When it is used up, Magpie must stop and wait for the next day.
Nothing is wrong with your account or with the app.

## How many channels

Magpie can move about 200 channels per day.
This is for everyone who uses Magpie together, not for each person.
On a busy day, you may move fewer.

## When the move can go on

The limit starts again once a day, at midnight Pacific Time (California time).
Magpie shows "Tomorrow" on the channels that are still waiting.
If you said yes to reminders, Magpie tells you when you can move the rest.

## What is safe

Channels that already moved stay moved.
Open Magpie and tap Continue to move the rest.
Nothing is lost, and no channel moves twice.
