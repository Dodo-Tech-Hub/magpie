# Magpie Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-11-10

Magpie is a mobile app made by Dodo Tech Hub ("we", "us"). It copies the list of YouTube channels you subscribe to from one Google account to another. This policy explains what Magpie does with your information.

**Short version:** Magpie has no server. Your information stays on your phone and goes only to Google. We do not see it, keep it, sell it, or share it.

## What Magpie accesses

When you sign in with Google, Magpie asks for permission to use your YouTube account. It uses two kinds of permission:

- **Old account (read-only):** Magpie reads the list of channels you subscribe to. It cannot change anything on this account.
- **New account (manage):** Magpie subscribes this account to the channels you pick.

For each account, Magpie also receives your Google account ID, name, email address, and profile photo. It shows them on screen so you can tell the accounts apart.

For each channel, Magpie receives the channel ID, name, and picture.

Magpie never sees your Google password. Sign-in is handled by Google's own sign-in tools on your phone.

## What Magpie stores on your phone

Magpie saves this on your phone only, so you can stop and continue later:

- The two accounts' ID, name, email, and photo link.
- The channels you chose to move, and whether each one is waiting, moved, already there, or could not be moved.
- Your choices in the app, such as the theme (Light, Dark, or Auto) and whether it already asked about reminders.

Magpie does not copy this to any cloud backup. Sign-in tokens are handled by Google's sign-in tools, and Magpie asks them for a token only when it needs to talk to YouTube.

## Where your information goes

Your information goes only to Google, so Magpie can read your subscriptions and add new ones. Magpie talks only to Google services.

Magpie has no server of its own. It has no analytics, no ads, and no crash reporting. We do not receive, sell, rent, or share your information with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising or to train AI models.

## Reminders

If you allow it, Magpie sends a reminder to your phone when the daily limit resets and you can move the rest of your channels. The reminder is made on your phone. It does not use a server. You can turn reminders off in your phone's settings at any time.

## How long we keep information, and how to delete it

Everything stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete it at any time:

- In Magpie, open Settings and tap "Delete all my data". Magpie removes everything it saved, signs you out, and removes its access to your Google account.
- Delete a single saved move from the list.
- Uninstall Magpie. This removes everything it saved.
- You can also remove Magpie's access at any time in your Google account at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
