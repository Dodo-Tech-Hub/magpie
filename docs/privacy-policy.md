# Magpie Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-11-10

Magpie is a mobile app made by Dodo Tech Hub ("we", "us"). It copies what you follow or subscribe to from one account to another on the platforms you connect. This policy explains what Magpie does with your information.

**Short version:** Magpie has no server. Your information stays on your phone and goes only to the platforms you connect. We do not see it, keep it, sell it, or share it.

## Platforms

Magpie works with the platforms listed in the "Platform details" section below. Each platform has its own rules and its own terms, and those are set out in that platform's section. This policy covers only the platforms listed there. When Magpie adds a platform, we add its section and update the date above before the platform is available.

## What Magpie accesses

When you connect an account, Magpie asks the platform for your permission. For each platform, Magpie uses these kinds of permission:

- **Old account (read-only):** Magpie reads the list of accounts or channels you follow or subscribe to. It cannot change anything on this account.
- **New account (manage):** Magpie follows or subscribes this account to the ones you pick.

For each account, Magpie also receives the account ID, name, email address, and profile photo, as far as the platform provides them. It shows them on screen so you can tell the accounts apart.

For each item you move, Magpie receives its ID, name, and picture.

Magpie never sees your passwords. Sign-in is handled by each platform's own sign-in tools on your phone.

## What Magpie stores on your phone

Magpie saves this on your phone only, so you can stop and continue later:

- The two accounts' ID, name, email, and photo link.
- The items you chose to move, and whether each one is waiting, moved, already there, or could not be moved.
- Your choices in the app, such as the theme (Light, Dark, or Auto) and whether it already asked about reminders.

Magpie does not copy this to any cloud backup. Sign-in tokens are handled by each platform's sign-in tools, and Magpie asks them for a token only when it needs to talk to that platform.

## Where your information goes

Your information goes only to the platforms you connect, so Magpie can read your list and add new items. Magpie talks only to those platforms.

Magpie has no server of its own. It has no analytics, no ads, and no crash reporting. We do not receive, sell, rent, or share your information with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising or to train AI models.

## Reminders

If you allow it, Magpie sends a reminder to your phone when a platform's daily limit resets and you can move the rest of your items. The reminder is made on your phone. It does not use a server. You can turn reminders off in your phone's settings at any time.

## How long we keep information, and how to delete it

Everything stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete it at any time:

- In Magpie, open Settings and tap "Delete all my data". Magpie removes everything it saved, signs you out, and removes its access to your accounts.
- Delete a single saved move from the list.
- Uninstall Magpie. This removes everything it saved.
- You can also remove Magpie's access at any time in each platform's own account settings. Each platform's section below says where.

## Children

Magpie is not made for children. You must be old enough to have an account on each platform you connect, under that platform's rules.

## Platform details

### Google and YouTube

Magpie uses YouTube API Services. When you connect a Google account, Magpie asks for permission to use your YouTube account, and it reads and writes your YouTube channel subscriptions. For the old account it asks for read-only permission, and for the new account it asks for manage permission. The account details above come from your Google account.

By using Magpie with Google and YouTube, you also agree to the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Magpie's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app follows the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

You can remove Magpie's access to your Google account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
